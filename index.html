ABOUT THIS DEMO

Built to lower the first hurdle.

Consumer Copilot is a prototype for the National Consumer Helpline journey. It is not an official government product. Sensitive information, routing, uploads, and docket numbers are mocked for this demo.

✓What is live
The conversational interface, structured case-building, browser voice fallback, optional Groq enhancement, and responsive UI.
◌What is mocked
Government submission, identity verification, image understanding, persistence, and case status. No government system is connected.
